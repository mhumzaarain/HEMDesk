# Track & confirm resolution

**Who:** Everyone — own complaints only

Use this to check on a complaint you lodged, and to confirm a repair actually fixed the problem.

1. From the sidebar, select **My Complaints**.
2. Find your complaint in the list and check its status:
    - **Open** — waiting for an engineer.
    - **Attached to Work Order** — a repair is underway.
    - **Closed** — see the close reason next to the status.

## Close reasons

- **Resolved** — the repair completed. This is set automatically; you don't do anything.
- **Duplicate** or **No Fault Found** — an engineer closed the complaint manually, or cancelled the work order it was attached to.

## Confirming a repair

When a repair resolves your complaint, the closed row asks **Is the machine functional now?** with two buttons: **Yes, functional** and **No, not functional**.

- Only the original reporter can answer.
- Your answer is recorded once and shown to engineers on the work order and the dashboard.
- Answering **No, not functional** does not automatically reopen anything — [lodge a new complaint](lodge.md) if the fault persists.
